Security Acknowledgments

Recognizing the researchers who help keep White Tiger Cyber and our clients secure through responsible disclosure.

Responsible Disclosure

Security is the work we do every day, and we hold our own systems to the same standard we set for our clients. If you discover a vulnerability affecting whitetigercyber.com or any of our services, we welcome your report and will work with you in good faith to resolve it.

Our machine-readable contact details follow the RFC 9116 standard and are published at /.well-known/security.txt.

How to Report

  1. Email your findings to info@whitetigercyber.com with enough detail for us to reproduce the issue.
  2. Give us a reasonable window to investigate and remediate before any public disclosure.
  3. Avoid privacy violations, service disruption, and accessing or modifying data that is not yours while testing.

We do not currently operate a paid bug-bounty program, but we are glad to publicly credit researchers who report valid issues responsibly — with your permission.

With Thanks

No disclosures have been credited yet — be the first. Researchers who responsibly report a valid vulnerability will be recognized here.
